How this quiz works

  • Single-answer MCQs (160): choose one option. Your answer locks immediately and instant feedback shows the correct answer with an explanation.
  • Scenario-based questions (60): apply concepts to practical situations. Each question has exactly one correct answer.
  • Scoring: 1 mark per question, no negative marking. Your answer is checked instantly.
  • Tools: Previous/Next, question palette, Mark for review, answer review, difficulty-wise results. Keyboard: A-D or 1-4 to answer, left/right arrows to navigate, M to mark.
