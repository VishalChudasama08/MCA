How this quiz works

  • Single-answer MCQs (70): choose one option. Your answer locks immediately and the correct answer with its explanation appears.
  • Scenario-based MSQs (30): select ALL correct answers. Correct picks turn green and wrong picks turn red. You are told how many correct answers are still missing, and the explanation appears only when your selection exactly matches. You can change your selection at any time.
  • Scoring: 1 mark per question, no negative marking. An MSQ scores only when your final selection matches the correct set exactly.
  • Tools: Previous/Next, question palette, Mark for review. Keys: A-D or 1-4 to select, left/right arrows to move, M to mark.
